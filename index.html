    Keyboard Shortcuts

    Alt+Ctrl+Space
    Take a screenshot
    Alt+Ctrl+R
    Start or stop screen recording
    Alt+Ctrl+D
    Turn the Capture Context Descriptor on or off

    Shortcuts are ignored while typing in a text field. Every action here is also available from an ordinary button.

    Change shortcuts

    These shortcuts work anywhere on Windows, even when another application has focus.

    Take Screenshot: Alt+Ctrl+Space
    Start or Stop Recording: Alt+Ctrl+R
    Toggle Capture Context Descriptor: Alt+Ctrl+D

    Capture Context Descriptor

    An independent, on-demand mode - not tied to taking a screenshot or recording. While on, it briefly announces the active application, window, and monitor whenever they meaningfully change, so you can understand your visual surroundings before deciding to capture anything. Off by default; stays on until you turn it off or exit the application.
